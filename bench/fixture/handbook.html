The Watch Handbook #

Standing orders for every officer, from Commander Vimes down. Read the oath before your first patrol.

Patrols #

A patrol is two officers. Nobody patrols the Shades alone, and nobody at all patrols it after midnight without a signed order from the duty sergeant.

  • Day patrol: six bells to noon
  • Night patrol: sunset to dawn
    • Cable Street: Angua and Carrot
    • Pseudopolis Yard: Detritus

Ringing the bell

Ring the bell twice for a fire and three times for a dragon. The call is:

watch bell --post "Cable Street" --times 3
watch report WATCH-12 --to vimes@example.com
